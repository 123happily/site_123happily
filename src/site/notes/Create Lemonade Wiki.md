It also works as a quick overview of what's different about Create: Lemonade if you're curious.


# Major Gameplay Changes

### The World

Create: Lemonade worlds loop around, letting you walk straight from spawn to eventually end up at spawn again. This makes them finite and 'completable'. Don't worry - the random seeds have been restricted to make sure you get all structures, all Vanilla biomes, a good mix of warm and cold regions, and at least one seriously tall mountain. There's plenty of room within the 10,240 block wide square for all your needs (that's bigger than most AAA game maps!).

**Therefore... Completable Map!**

The atlas item lets you take a map wall and keep it in your pocket. Mark waypoints with banners (did you know that's a Vanilla feature?) and try to map the whole world.
