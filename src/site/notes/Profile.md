I respect the importance of customer satisfaction and relationships, and maintain a focus on this throughout my work.

##### **Minecraft World Generation Datapacks**

The popular computer game Minecraft allows users to modify many portions of its code. I became interested in the process of distilling various random noises to something resembling real terrain, and began teaching myself the skills necessary to produce my own modifications. Working within the limitations of .json files to achieve a final vision required multiple create-test-change iterative cycles. I also wrote explanations for end users to support adoption and ongoing modification.

The first pack I produced creates terrain from scratch with several layered noise maps, customisable by the end user with minimal effort.
